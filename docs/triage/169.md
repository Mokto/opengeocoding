# Unresolved gold locations: evidence before imports

Investigation for [#169](https://github.com/Mokto/opengeocoding/issues/169), 2026-10-18. The baseline investigation below adopted no source and changed no runtime behavior. The runtime follow-up at the end fixes four retrieval failures without changing the builder, source inputs, reference data, expectations, tolerances or known-failure labels.

## Reproduction and disposition

The issue lists **226 training cases at 213 location keys**, not all 279 historical failures. All 226 still fail SearchAddress on current main, `f7a6727a611b`: 134 `too_coarse`, 47 `near_miss`, 30 `abstain`, 15 `far_miss`. Their expected levels are 125 streets, 92 houses, 5 localities, 3 postcodes and 1 region. The historical 279 and these counts have different scopes. Held-out cases were scored in the full gate, not inspected or used to choose a source or mechanism.

[169-locations.tsv](169-locations.tsv) records every listed training case, its existing failure class, reference, expected point, attribution and bounded index/upstream evidence:

| Attribution | Cases |
|---|---:|
| Present-but-unretrieved | 44 |
| Confirmed source/import absence | 1 |
| Unsupported/underspecified input | 20 |
| Reference defect | 1 |
| Still unresolved | 160 |

These are conservative investigation dispositions, **not 226 resolved bugs or promised passes**. “Present-but-unretrieved” includes a returned street whose representative point fails the tolerance, and a damaged query whose intended feature is present but whose acceptance still needs review. It does not establish that relaxing a guard would be correct. The 160 unresolved cases remain unresolved, not relabelled missing data. Country totals and numbers of house keys are not address-level evidence or national accuracy rates.

### What changed the diagnosis

- The exact expected house keys are present for Furrenstrasse 24, Melchnaustrasse 41a, Rifugio 376, Austrasse 105, Geuenseestrasse 33, Monti di Cortói 15, the two Spanish damaged-address locations, Le Thanh Tong 1, Tran Phu 50, Nguyễn Công Trứ 16a, Riebeek Street 2 and Escoda Street 962. The table gives street ids, key distances and upstream rows. Further importing their reference registries would not fix retrieval.
- Köpmangatan, Nguyễn Trãi and Mederov have indexed lines inside the expected radius while the returned street point lies outside it. Investigate street identity, merging and representative-point selection separately from coverage. A street centroid is not proof that its line is absent near the reference.
- The region BA-BIH exists, with its full English name. `B&H` does not retrieve it. Shuikou exists at the reference point, but another Overture place, Feiluan 89 km away, carries the same Wikidata id and Chinese Shuikou names. Those are abbreviation/scoping and source-name-contamination opportunities, not grounds to import another region or town.
- A district, village, postcode, P.O. box or named campus without a road does not support a street-level expectation merely because Wikidata also supplies a point. SearchAddress does not return landmarks. This accounts for 20 input/granularity mismatches; expectations were **not** weakened to make them pass.
- The Anglia Ruskin query explicitly names Bishop Hall Lane, Chelmsford CM1 1SQ, but its gold point is the university's Cambridge point. [Wikidata Q540751 revision 2511564675](https://www.wikidata.org/w/index.php?title=Q540751&oldid=2511564675) has P625 `(52.203083333333, 0.13369444444444)` and Cambridge headquarters. [ARU's Chelmsford campus page](https://www.aru.ac.uk/student-life/life-on-campus/chelmsford-campus), checked 2026-10-18, confirms the query's Chelmsford address. This needs a campus-specific authoritative reference/seed repair, not matching tuned to Cambridge. No replacement point is inferred from this service.
- NAD_r24 OID `86231764`, UUID `{FB3C05E8-037E-4747-A906-12CEDE0CE1EE}`, contains 7400, 205TH Lane Northeast at `(48.181880212, -122.130260638)`. A full number-FST coordinate scan finds no `7400` within 1 km, independently of the reverse files; the bounded inspection finds 74 local street records and no 205th Lane. This confirms a local indexed feature gap. **NAD cannot be imported to fix it: NAD_r24 is already the US registry gold source.** It is not an independently eligible addition even though its terms permit reuse.

`evaluate inspect` reads the stored simplified edges within 1 km, every house-bearing street cell covering that radius, and **all number keys on those streets**, without relevance ranking, country/region pruning or a nearest-N limit. It does not call libpostal or a service. A line may cross the circle with its centroid far outside it. Distances are local planar approximations over the index's quantized geometry (several metres of error), not surveyed measurements. Points at latitude ±85° or beyond, and windows crossing the antimeridian, are rejected.

This is not an automatic absence classifier. It relies on the builder's reverse files, does not inspect polygon interiors or postcode/place documents, and cannot find a street that has neither a stored line nor local houses. A feature outside the 1 km window, a different alias, a broken reverse posting, an incorrectly placed reference, or a correct conflict guard can explain a negative result. The confirmed house absence above was additionally checked against **all FSTs**, not inferred from empty reverse results. Postcode cases explicitly remain unresolved pending full postcode/source-row inspection.

For the source side, all Overture address and named-road segment rows whose bounding boxes intersect a window of ±0.01° latitude and ±`0.01 / cos(latitude)` longitude around each training point were read. This is a bounding-box candidate window, not an assertion that every source line is within 1 km. The TSV's source distance is to the original geometry, while the index distance is to its simplified geometry. A row attached to an unresolved case is a **representative neighbour, not a verified match to its intended feature**. A positive street/house attribution uses the named stored feature; its upstream row is matched by name (and number for an address row). Division evidence was checked separately against `places.tsv` and the pinned division parquet.

The local index has no sealed input manifest identifying every original byte. Its `meta.json` digest identifies its segments, not a hash of every sidecar. The builder hash, saved import report, source release and row identities bound this investigation; do not silently substitute another index or source snapshot and treat the conclusions as reproduced.

## Bounded recommendation: northern Snohomish OSM address nodes

Investigate a **small data-only pilot**, not a nationwide import: addressed OSM nodes in the rectangle longitude `[-122.20, -122.05]`, latitude `[48.05, 48.25]`, in the Marysville–Arlington area of Washington, US. This bounds the estimate; it is not a county boundary. No new fetch/import implementation is adopted by this PR.

Source: [Geofabrik Washington PBF](https://download.geofabrik.de/north-america/us/washington.html), downloaded 2026-10-18 from `washington-latest.osm.pbf`, header replication sequence **4942**, timestamp **2026-10-16T20:21:21Z**, 364,703,390 bytes, SHA-256:

```text
46cdc107cf14cefa08be50dc9db4bf24adcc9c9952c8244674cf2fd937828303
```

The estimate uses **source rows, not gold failures**. Only point features with nonempty `addr:street` and `addr:housenumber` were counted; ways, relations and interpolation were not estimated. The checked-in [measurement example](../../examples/triage_169_overlap.rs) reads no gold corpus:

| Measurement | Keys/rows |
|---|---:|
| Addressed source nodes in the rectangle | 2,280 rows |
| Distinct `(fold(street), number_key(number))` pairs | 930 keys |
| Existing same-name/number key within 100 m | 871 |
| Existing same-name/number key, but farther away | 13 |
| No same-name/number key in the larger index window | 46 |
| Of those, simple digit + optional single-letter numbers | 40 |
| After excluding any same-number indexed house within 100 m, irrespective of road spelling | **29 candidate keys** |

The index comparison scans all FST keys in longitude `[-122.7, -120.8]`, latitude `[47.7, 48.5]`, folding every stored road alias with this code's analyzer. These are estimated **candidate additions**, not verified distinct dwellings or 29 expected gold passes. Text-key collisions, bad source tags, multiple points for one key, polygon assignment and importer filtering can reduce the result. The 13 distant conflicts are quarantined, not “new houses”. Six embedded-unit/non-simple keys are excluded; seven further simple keys have a nearby same-number house under another road spelling. No candidate-level gold outcome was used to select these filters.

The low measured ceiling argues against a broad import solely to improve the selected failures. First manually validate the 29 nodes and their edit/source histories; most addressed nodes in the whole window have no explicit source tag (2,278 of 2,280). OSM is not the NAD registry used to generate US gold, but absent source tags do **not** establish independent underlying acquisition. Reject NAD-derived nodes or redesign the evaluation with an independently sourced reference before adoption. Do not promise independent accuracy from provider names alone.

### Terms, precedence and adoption gate

- OSM's [ODbL 1.0](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) permits commercial reuse and redistribution subject to attribution and applicable database share-alike obligations; it is already a distributed input of this project. An adopted extract must retain “© OpenStreetMap contributors”, the ODbL notice, Geofabrik provenance, the frozen PBF checksum/header and the extraction bounds/filter. Do not treat the source-available code licence as the data licence.
- Keep existing source priority: Overture addresses before OpenAddresses before OSM. Match the builder's country/place/normalized-road grouping and exact number key. Existing higher-priority coordinates/postcodes win; new nodes fill absent keys only. Check aliases and same-number spatial duplicates before import; quarantine conflicting coordinates rather than replacing a known point. The measurement is deliberately more conservative than the importer's exact-key deduplication.
- Never import NAD_r24 or another registry used as that country's gold truth without an evaluation redesign. The inspected Snohomish NAD subset has 320,040 rows, but it is **rejected**, not an eligible gain estimate. The [USDOT disclaimer](https://www.transportation.gov/gis/national-address-database/national-address-database-nad-disclaimer) permits reuse under 17 USC §105 and warns against mailing-list use; permissive terms do not repair evaluation leakage.
- The Egyptian, Bangkok and South African OA files were skipped as `Unknown` in this index's import report. That is an import-policy fact, not evidence that every selected address is absent. In particular, [Cape Town's linked terms](https://www.capetown.gov.za/General/Terms-of-use-open-data), checked 2026-10-18, do not clearly state a redistribution grant and reference an Esri agreement. Do not bypass the licence filter or blanket-allow these sources to chase failures.
- An eventual pilot is a **separate data/import experiment**: base index versus rebuilt candidate from otherwise identical frozen inputs, same consumer/models/references, stable repeat, full gold diff including held-out evaluation, and independent source-history review. Keep resolver changes out of that comparison. Reject every newly failing/worse-class case, including abstain-to-wrong-answer. Update the chart hash and local index when hash-covered code changes; a source-only experiment still needs separately identified index/input fingerprints. No builder hash change is needed for this diagnostic/report-only PR.

## Reproduce the inspection

Build `evaluate`, then select only the IDs in the TSV from the gold suite; reject held-out rows rather than silently dropping them:

```sh
target/release/evaluate gold-corpus --out /tmp/169-gold.jsonl
python3 - <<'PY'
import csv, json
ids = {r['id'] for r in csv.DictReader(open('docs/triage/169-locations.tsv'), delimiter='\t')}
cases = [json.loads(line) for line in open('/tmp/169-gold.jsonl')]
selected = [c for c in cases if c['id'] in ids]
assert len(selected) == len(ids) == 226
assert all(not c['holdout'] for c in selected)
with open('/tmp/169-training.jsonl', 'w') as out:
    for case in selected:
        out.write(json.dumps(case, ensure_ascii=False) + '\n')
PY
target/release/evaluate inspect --index "$INDEX_DIR" \
  --corpus /tmp/169-training.jsonl --out /tmp/169-index-evidence.jsonl
```

Inspect the cited source rows in the `2026-09-23.1` address/segment/division parquet, including their `sources`, `version` and full geometry. To repeat the bounded source-window join with DuckDB and its `spatial` extension, set `DATA` to that release directory:

```sh
duckdb <<SQL
LOAD spatial;
CREATE TEMP TABLE points AS
SELECT id case_id, expect.point[1] lat, expect.point[2] lon
FROM read_json_auto('/tmp/169-training.jsonl');
COPY (
  SELECT c.case_id, s.id, s.names, s.version, s.sources, ST_AsText(s.geometry) geometry
  FROM read_parquet('$DATA/segment/*') s JOIN points c
  ON s.bbox.xmin <= c.lon + 0.01/cos(radians(c.lat))
  AND s.bbox.xmax >= c.lon - 0.01/cos(radians(c.lat))
  AND s.bbox.ymin <= c.lat + 0.01 AND s.bbox.ymax >= c.lat - 0.01
  WHERE s.subtype = 'road' AND s.names.primary IS NOT NULL
) TO '/tmp/169-source-segments.jsonl' (FORMAT JSON);
SQL
```

Use the same join for the address rows, selecting `street` and `number` instead of `names` and removing the segment subtype/name filter. Empty windows do not establish source absence. For the OSM estimate, preserve the pinned PBF locally (the `latest` URL changes), verify its SHA-256, then:

```sh
osmium tags-filter washington.osm.pbf n/addr:housenumber -R -o wa-nodes.osm.pbf
osmium export wa-nodes.osm.pbf --geometry-types=point \
  -a type,id,version,timestamp -f geojsonseq -o wa-nodes.geojsonseq
cargo run --release --locked --example triage_169_overlap -- "$INDEX_DIR" wa-nodes.geojsonseq
cargo test --locked --example triage_169_overlap
```

## Validation

Two independent full gold runs on the same unchanged consumer/index/models/references established zero response drift over **8,934 cases**. All four RPCs had zero changed results. Search passed 7,654/8,855, suggestions 6,055/8,936 checks, region 3,312/3,333, reverse 7,680/7,801. The full diff, including newly failing and worse failure classes, was empty. The known grpcurl SearchAddress failure reproduced. Timing improved with cache warming in the second run of the **same binary**; this is not a speedup claim.

The authored inspection regression creates 12 nearby roads and a road line whose stored centroid is far away. It requires all 12 roads and the target number key, beyond a nearest-five/top-ten probe. A separate check rejects held-out inspection. The overlap example checks its simple-number filter. No named gold location is encoded in service logic, and the recognized-country/region guards, postcode-area compatibility, fallback ordering and `good_match` semantics are untouched.

The initial diagnostic-only head evaluator was also run against the same unchanged service binary, index, models and references: another full 8,934-case comparison with `--fail-on-drift` passed, with zero changed results and no newly failing or worse-class cases. The entire diff and its speed section were reviewed; the local speed gate passed, without a performance claim from these warmed runs.

Initial diagnostic-only checks passed: `cargo fmt --check`, `cargo clippy --locked --all-targets -- -D warnings` (Homebrew Rust/Clippy 1.99.1), `cargo test --locked`, the inspection synthetic tests, the overlap example test, and the pinned overlap measurement above. Native checks used `LIBRARY_PATH=/opt/homebrew/lib`; the first build lacked that path and failed to link libpostal, then passed with it. The default Clippy invocation selected an old rustup nightly and failed its minimum-Rust check; invoking Homebrew's `cargo-clippy` with its matching `RUSTC` passed. Docker and the ignored RPC integration suite were not run: no runtime RPC/lifecycle code changed. Full gold reproduced the existing grpcurl failure rather than suppressing it.

The diagnostic-only CI quality comment reported zero changed results. Its speed comment failed despite identifying the same service binary on both sides; that comparison does not establish a runtime change. No source is adopted, and the 160 still-unresolved attributions remain outstanding.

## Runtime follow-up: four retrieval failures fixed

Against main `dbd46f3`, the existing country-scoped house-query expansion now also handles dotted German `str.` compounds with a two-letter-or-longer stem (Switzerland, Germany and Austria), one-edit errors in a final compound `strasse` suffix, and a final Afrikaans `Straat`/English `Street` in South Africa. A misspelt long suffix must be repaired before the index analyzer shortens an intact compound to `str`; correcting against the already-shortened vocabulary could not find it.

The existing safety conditions remain: only after an exact street miss, only with a recognized country and a resolved town or indexed postcode, only an exact stored house number, no conflicting house postcode, and no expansion when alternatives identify different street documents. Literal street matches retain precedence. Country/region conflict guards and resolver fallback ordering are unchanged. No broad fuzzy search, global street-name merge, source import, schema change or index rebuild is introduced.

The same full local index (`358185ba7395`, builder `9547c5f06d71e087`) and models (`65627ea2114b`) were used for both sides. Consumer binaries were `4aef33c9ac30` (base) and `9f31f41dad3c` (candidate), with identical reference data and corpus digest `8c0c26fcaa59f08fc0ee7b805edd3a20f01720e2b84074e99623dfe41c74591d`. Two base runs established zero response drift before comparing the change. Among 8,934 shared cases, SearchAddress improves from **7,654 to 7,658 / 8,855**, with exactly four changed responses, all `too_coarse` → `pass`:

|---|---|---:|

| `degraded-melchnausrasse-41a-4934-madiswil-switzerland` | Melchnaustrasse 41a | under 1 m |

| `address-2-riebeek-straat-foreshore-cape-town-8001-south-` | Riebeek Street 2 | 177 m |

All four now return the requested number and postcode with `good_match=true`, under the existing expectations and tolerances. None is held out. The full diff has **no newly failing or worse-class cases**, including abstain-to-wrong-answer changes. SuggestAddress, ResolveRegion and ReverseGeocode have zero changed responses. A separate base/head comparison of all 1,000 address snapshot cases also has zero response drift; no accepted snapshot was replaced.

Authored made-up roads exercise compound typos, a short dotted compound, Afrikaans/English road types, literal-name precedence, and rejection of conflicting country/postcode and missing numbers through the real builder, parser and resolver. `cargo fmt --check`, Clippy for all targets, `cargo test --locked`, and `cargo test --locked --test synthetic -- --ignored --nocapture` pass. `cargo test --locked --test rpc -- --ignored --nocapture` has passing search/guard checks and diagnostics but fails its CDG landmark suggestion assertion on this local index; the unchanged base service reproduces the same assertion. The known grpcurl SearchAddress failure is still reproduced in full evaluation. No assertion was skipped or weakened.

The inspector also rejects a location assigned to hold-out by the stable location hash even when its supplied flag is false or absent; its regression checks both routes.

The local speed gate is **not established**: the final base/head comparison exceeds its budget (SearchAddress total +21%, p90 +38%, p99 +33%; SuggestAddress p90 +14%, p99 +44%; ReverseGeocode total +16%, p90 +20%, p99 +92%). A base-versus-itself comparison also fails several timing thresholds despite zero response drift. These shared-machine runs cannot attribute the observed timing differences to this change; the CI quality and speed comments still need review. The remaining investigation cases and missing/unsupported-data mechanisms are not claimed fixed.
