# Historical membership observations

Two compiled-in files hold dated membership observations, and one country-neutral matcher reads both:

- `data/historical_memberships.json`: hand-reviewed observations, each with its own sources and review (one settlement in the Philippines).
- `data/historical_memberships.tsv` with `data/historical_sources.json`: about 31,000 observations generated by `update_history` from official administrative datasets, [listed below](#imported-datasets-216). Each row expands into the same observation format at startup and passes the same validator.

Neither is a raw Wikidata import or a complete worldwide history. Adding a country takes a reviewed source adapter or reviewed record, not a country branch in the resolver.

SearchAddress consults these observations **only when its existing lookups and retries found no good match**: an abstention, or a fallback answered with `good_match=false` (such as `Moss, Viken, Norway` answering the hamlet Viken in Østfold). A current interpretation with `good_match=true` retains precedence. Recovery accepts exactly three comma-separated parts: an exact indexed settlement name, the historical qualifier, and a recognized country. The settlement may use an existing indexed alternate name. Partial names, numeric town/qualifier parts, additional address fields and qualifiers naming a recognized country are excluded. A separately recognized foreign country in the settlement part cannot be overridden.

A recovered answer has the settlement's **current** indexed coordinates, address components and region codes. `good_match=true` means this complete place query is corroborated by an observation; no requested street, house or postcode was discarded. This does not offer historical coordinates, an as-of-date RPC, historical street matching or historical-country interpretation. SuggestAddress, ReverseGeocode, ResolveRegion and ListRegions do not use these observations.

## File and review contract

Each record asserts that the entire listed membership path held at `observed_on`. This is a point observation, not a claim that it held continuously between that date and today. Facts from separate dates must not be combined into an invented chain.

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `id` | Unique, stable observation identifier. |
| `country` | Uppercase present-day country code; all bound indexed places must agree. |
| `observed_on` | Gregorian `YYYY-MM-DD` at which the reviewed path is supported. |
| `reviewed_on` | UTC review date, no earlier than the observation or source retrieval and no later than the server's UTC day. |
| `path` | Settlement first, any unchanged lower ancestors next, historical ancestor last. Nodes have a source-backed `name` and Wikidata identity. |
| `sources` | Public evidence URLs, retrieval dates, optional publication dates and the zero-based path edges they support. Every edge needs evidence. |
| `review` | Why these sources establish the common dated path and identity links, and what they do not establish. |

A generated row (`snapshot`, `code`, `name`, `wikidata`, `ancestor`, `observed_on`) becomes the observation `snapshot/code/observed_on` with the path `[name (wikidata), the snapshot's reviewed ancestor for that code]`; its country, `reviewed_on` and review are the snapshot's, and the snapshot's membership files are the evidence for edge `0`. The snapshot in `data/historical_sources.json` also records its licence, attribution, each input's SHA-256 and retrieval date, and the Wikidata query that linked the codes.

For `[settlement, municipality, province, former region]`, edge `0` is settlement → municipality, edge `1` municipality → province, and edge `2` province → former region. A Wikidata identifier identifies an entity; it is not by itself proof of its historical membership.

Binding happens at startup against existing place rows. The settlement must be a locality/local administration with the recorded exact name in the recorded country, or a county row carrying the settlement's own reviewed Wikidata ID (Overture files Norway's municipalities as counties). Indexed Wikidata IDs must agree with the reviewed ones. A settlement lacking an indexed ID must be a locality/local administration and requires **two adjacent, named and identified lower ancestors**, in the same country, matching the reviewed path. An absent middle ancestor, changed lower chain, explicit conflicting identity, malformed/cyclic current chain or multiple matching place rows prevents recovery. The historical last ancestor need not exist in today's index. Distinct or ambiguous bindings for the requested name and qualifier abstain, even when one candidate is larger or an alternate spelling appears unique. Repeated observations of the same uniquely identified place do not create multiple answers.

This does not attach a province's old parent to every current descendant. A newly created or later-arriving town without its own reviewed observation gets no history from this file. A known-ID settlement can have a two-node observation directly naming a former ancestor; an unlinked settlement cannot use that shortcut. Relocations or changed lower ancestry of an unlinked settlement require new identity evidence, not relaxed binding.

The files are versioned and reviewed with code. Invalid schemas, dates, IDs or incomplete evidence fail loading. Missing or ambiguous identities in a regional/different source snapshot simply remain unavailable for recovery. There is no runtime file override, network access, download or write. The evidence is neither merged into settlement aliases nor written into index ancestry. No builder/index format or builder hash changes; older indexes can be used if their existing identities bind.

The evaluator records `historical_memberships_sha256` alongside its existing reference fingerprint. Since #216 this hashes all three compiled files together (earlier manifests hashed the JSON file alone), the evaluator's own copies, not an RPC attestation of a separately supplied `SERVICE_BINARY`; binary/data control experiments must also record what each executable embedded. Hashing embedded bytes needs no additional data file in evaluation images. Treat additions as data updates: inspect primary sources, retain provenance/attribution, review scoped identities and dates, and compare the effect separately from runtime changes. A reference URL, statement rank or parse success alone is not approval. Raw source files and candidate extracts remain outside the repository.

## Imported datasets (#216)

`cargo run --locked --bin update_history -- CACHE_DIR [--refresh]` regenerates `data/historical_sources.json` and `data/historical_memberships.tsv`. Each source has an adapter in [`src/bin/update_history.rs`](../../src/bin/update_history.rs): it reads one official release, keeps the settlement code, the settlement name, the ancestor code and the observation date of every membership, and links the settlement to a Wikidata item through the dataset's own code. A row is the observation `[settlement, ancestor]` on that date, supported by the release; the runtime expands it into the observation format above and validates it like a hand-reviewed record.

| Snapshot | Official source and licence | Observed on | Ancestors imported | Settlement identity | Rows | Bound |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| `no-ssb-2017` | Statistics Norway KLASS municipality (131) → county (104) correspondence, CC BY 4.0 | 2017-12-31 | Sør- and Nord-Trøndelag (the others repeat 2019) | municipality number, P2504 | 47 | 31 |
| `no-ssb-2019` | same | 2019-12-31 | all 18 counties but Oslo | P2504 | 416 | 324 |

| `fr-insee-cog-2015` | Insee Code officiel géographique 2015, communes and regions, Licence Ouverte 2.0 | 2015-01-01 | the 17 regions the 2016 reform merged or renamed | Insee code, P374 | 30,088 | 29,161 |
| `it-anpr` | ANPR archivio storico dei comuni (Ministero dell'Interno with Istat), CC BY 4.0 | each interval's last day | the 14 provinces a comune has left since 2000 | cadastral code, P806 | 340 | 339 |

"Bound" counts rows whose identity, name and country match exactly one index row of the local index (builder `9d4006a4eec15abb`), by an offline check; the service itself logs `bound=30202` of 31,247 observations, the Caliling record included. Parsing, validating and binding them took 0.3 s of a 4.4 s index load on the development host.

### Identity and date rules

- **Codes are read at the observation date.** A settlement code links to the Wikidata items whose non-deprecated statement for that code holds on the date (start and end qualifiers, when present). Exactly one item is required: codes held by several items on that date (1,082 French codes, mostly a commune nouvelle that kept a merged commune's code with neither statement dated; 12 Italian cadastral codes) and codes without an item (4 Norwegian) are left out, and the importer prints the counts.
- **Ancestors come from reviewed tables** in the adapter, keyed by the dataset's code at that date, with the dataset's name checked against the table wherever the dataset has one (KLASS county names, split at their Sami forms; Insee's 2015 region file). The Italian archive has plate codes but no province names, so the Istat code and plate code of the time key the table. Codes are not timeless: Istat 104 was Olbia-Tempio until 2016 and is Gallura Nord-Est Sardegna now; Insee region 83 has two Wikidata items, the 2015 region and the historical province; Wikidata's ISO 3166-2 dates for Norway's counties (2020-11-24) are not their legal dates. A source code missing from a table stops the import.
- **Settlement names are the dataset's**: KLASS's disambiguating suffix and Sami second name are dropped ("Herøy (Nordland)" → "Herøy", "Kautokeino - Guovdageaidnu" → "Kautokeino"), Insee's article is joined ("L'Abergement-Clémenciat"), Italian names stay as published in capitals. Binding folds case and accents as the index does.
- **Selection.** Regions or counties that kept their territory and name (ten French regions, 5,488 communes; Oslo, which is its own municipality and county) are not imported. Italian intervals are imported when they end in 2000 or later in a province other than the comune's current one, or, for a suppressed comune, in a province that no longer exists.
- Each row is supported at its own date by its own release. Rows of different dates are never chained, and only the listed settlements carry history: a commune created after 2015, or a municipality absent from a snapshot, gets none from it.

### Updating a source

1. `cargo run --locked --bin update_history -- ~/opengeocoding-history-cache` (network access and `unzip` on the path) reproduces the checked-in files from the cached inputs; without `--refresh`, an input whose bytes differ from its recorded SHA-256 stops the import, so the files are always generated from the pinned inputs. A missing cache file is downloaded again and must match.
2. `--refresh` downloads every input again (the Wikidata identity responses change daily) and records the new checksums and retrieval dates. `reviewed_on` is the latest retrieval date of a snapshot's inputs, so a rerun on the same inputs is byte-identical.
3. Review the diff as a data update: rows that appear or disappear, the skip counts printed per snapshot, and any ancestor code a new release adds (it stops the import until a reviewed table entry names it).
4. A new country needs a source with a dated, reusable licence, a code that Wikidata carries, an adapter and a reviewed ancestor table; add its attribution to `NOTICE`.
5. Run the unit tests and compare the gold suite on the same index, separately from any runtime change.

### Not imported

- **Philippines (PSGC).** psa.gov.ph refuses automated downloads (HTTP 403) and its reuse terms for the PSGC files were not established. The hand-reviewed Caliling observation stays; Negros Occidental's other barangays have no history.
- **Vietnam's 2025 province mergers.** The index already has the 34 new provinces, and old qualifiers fail often (`Thủ Dầu Một, Bình Dương, Vietnam` abstains). No openly licensed, machine-readable official list was found, and most indexed former cities lack the identities a binding needs.
- **Sud Sardegna.** The ANPR archive still lists it as current while the index has the 2025 Sardinian provinces, so it is not historical according to the source.
- No adapter yet for the UK's 1996 counties (Avon, Cleveland, Humberside), India (2000, 2014, 2019), Indonesia (2012, 2022), Chile (2007, 2018), Kenya (2013) or earlier reforms of the imported countries.
- A settlement whose name folds to a country code (Ås → AS, Ål → AL) is read as a foreign country by the existing guard and does not recover.
- **Homonyms answered with `good_match=true` keep precedence.** The existing matcher ignores an unmatched qualifier when the place name is otherwise unique enough: in a 400-row sample of the French rows, 28 queries (`<commune>, <2015 region>, France`) answer a namesake elsewhere with `good_match=true`, which this change leaves unchanged. Letting a bound observation override such an answer would be a separate change to current-lookup precedence.

### Caliling, Cauayan, Negros Occidental, Western Visayas — July 2021

The [Philippine government report published 25 July 2021](https://www.ntfelcac.gov.ph/post/news-pna-1148234) explicitly locates Barangay Caliling in Cauayan, Negros Occidental. Its event-day caption is inconsistent with its publication date; the evidence used is the geographic membership described in July 2021, not an inferred exact day of the event.

[EO 38](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/5/78545) restored Negros Occidental to Region VI in 2017. Section 7 makes newspaper publication the effectivity condition; the Supreme Court catalogue records 10 August 2017 publication. [Western Visayas (Q13665)](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q13665) is Region VI. This supports the 2021 chain without assuming that today's descendants all belonged to that region then.

[Caliling Q31528302 revision 2090431456](https://www.wikidata.org/w/index.php?title=Q31528302&oldid=2090431456) identifies Caliling and GeoNames 1709625. The existing index row has no Wikidata ID, so binding requires the recorded Cauayan Q195287 → Negros Occidental Q13862 lower chain, its exact source-backed names and a unique settlement. The query's Kaliling spelling is already an indexed alternate name; this file does not introduce it.

[EO 183](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/5/62394) had established NIR in 2015 before its 2017 revocation. [RA 12000](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/97490) re-established NIR in 2024, with effectivity dependent on complete publication. Neither the Act's approval date nor Wikidata's 13 June endpoint is substituted for a verified legal transition date. The observation claims no exact endpoint or uninterrupted membership interval. Outputs retain today's Negros Island Region/province ancestry and codes.

### Drammen municipality, Viken — 1 January 2021

Statistics Norway's [date-scoped KLASS correspondence](https://data.ssb.no/api/klass/v1/classifications/131/correspondsAt?targetClassificationId=104&date=2021-01-01) explicitly maps municipality `3005` Drammen (classification `131`) to county `30` Viken (classification `104`). These are historical classification identifiers, not timeless codes.

The reviewed entity links are Drammen **municipality** Q26525 and Viken Q56407178, not the separately identified urban settlement. The municipal identity must exist in the index; a name-only match cannot substitute the urban settlement. Viken need not still exist in the index. Current interpretations retain precedence, so an already answered Drammen query is not replaced by this path.

The historical lookup is live and can receive corrections. The response inspected on 19 October 2026 has SHA-256 `3ee6af63350e4216b5225c66ff0a7a74f06221469bf8e6a1b8d520accefebfea`. Statistics Norway's [CC BY 4.0 reuse policy](https://www.ssb.no/en/diverse/lisens) applies; attribution and the reduction/linking of its data are recorded in `NOTICE`. This observation does not establish unchanged boundaries or full membership endpoints.
