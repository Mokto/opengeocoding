# Houses SuggestAddress finds and SearchAddress misses

Triage for [issue #358](https://github.com/Mokto/opengeocoding/issues/358): OSM-panel cases where SearchAddress fails while SuggestAddress lists the expected house among its first five.

## Reproduction

`main` at `7f02d3f` on the local world index (builder `cd477fbcc95de70c`, the inputs of `~/opengeocoding-index/build.sh`), one request per case over the gold suite. The panel has **1,313** such cases here; the issue's 1,127 come from the nightly, on another index. 248 of them are held out: they count in the totals and the gate, but were not read or attributed. The other **1,065** are attributed one by one in [358-cases.tsv](358-cases.tsv), with their class on that base and after all three changes below (`class_after`).

Each case was replayed with the resolver's parse and every tier's index candidates logged, and assigned to the first step that loses the house:

| Step | What happens | Cases | Pass after this change |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Parse | libpostal does not read the query's number as the house number (491), or reads no road (45) | 536 | 120 |
| Place corroboration | The index search returns the house, and candidate selection rejects it | 399 | 107 |
| Street lookup | The parse is right; no street candidate | 78 | 11 |
| House lookup | The street is found, not the number | 52 | 2 |

## Groups with one cause

Training cases, across steps:

| Cause | Example | Cases | Pass after |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| The postcode before the country is read as the house number, the country as a road; the parse keeps nothing else. `country_as_road` repairs it only when the house carries that postcode | `Св. Спасо Радовишки 32, Радовиш, 2420, North Macedonia` | 65 | 45 |
| A calling code where the postcode goes (`+993`, `855`, `975`); the data often gives the house that "postcode" too | `Rangjung 13, 975, Bhutan` | 46 | 35 |
| The house is filed under a county named like the stated town, with no town of its own, which corroborates nothing | `661 Rue de la Décolonisation, Libreville, Estuaire, Gabon` | 57 | 5 |
| The country is not parsed; the town and country are read as a house name | `Rue Christophe 2, Saint-Michel de L'Atalaye, Haiti` | 25 | 15 |
| libpostal does not know `Palestinian Territory` as a country | `10 شارع القدس, دورا, Palestinian Territory` | 21 | 0 |

The rest are smaller patterns, often one country's, for example Hong Kong's bilingual roads parsed with the number inside the road (`陶樂路 To Lok Road 8`), British postcodes split into a postcode and a town (`YO30 2BF` as `yo30` and `2bf`), and Bahraini road and block numbers.

## Change: the completion lookup for town and empty answers

`suggested_house` (from [#244](https://github.com/Mokto/opengeocoding/pull/244)) already asked the completion lookup, which reads no parse, for the house when the answer was a postcode, a street, or a town whose parse read no number. A town or empty answer whose parse read a number was excluded, and most of the cases above end exactly there. It is now included, with the same checks on the house (its number, every word of its street, its town or postcode and its country written in the query; no other number unexplained; one house within 200 m), and one more: since such an answer gives nothing to check the house against, every comma part of the query has to be explained by the house's road, places, country or numbers, or name a place or country. `5a Orchard Road, Missing Bay, Harbourton` stays unanswered.

A part written with a plus (`+993`) is now a calling code in every country, not only the African ones listed with their codes, and a house whose postcode is that code answers without it, as those countries' houses already did.

## Second change: a county named for its seat

The index search returns `661, Rue de la Décolonisation` for `661 Rue de la Décolonisation, Libreville, Estuaire, Gabon`, filed under the county (department) Libreville and no town, so the stated town corroborated nothing. A house is now in the stated town when a county above it is named so, a town of that name lies inside that county (its seat), the stated state names a region above it, the postcode does not disagree, and the house's postal town, if it has one, is that town. The last condition keeps the synthetic `32 Courier Road, Posthaven`, whose postal town is Farhaven, unconfident; it also leaves unanswered two Belgrade register cases and four panel cases (Lubumbashi, Dakar) whose houses carry another postal town.

Same index, base `main` at `6b838bf` (the first change): SearchAddress 32,973 → 33,001 (+28: 17 Gabon, 9 China, 2 Venezuela), nothing newly failing or worse, panel search-fail/suggest-pass 1,025 → 997. Of the 57 training cases in this group, 9 more pass; most of the other 43 fail at the parse first (`Rue Joséphine Kengue 162, Montalier, Libreville` reads `montalier` as the road, `Williams Way 247, Harare` keeps the number in the road).

## Third change: the groups with one cause each

Same index, base `main` at `3ebf5b3` (the second change), all 39,899 gold cases (the Hong Kong register corpus included):

- **Names in two scripts.** `屯門 tuen mun` (lowercased by the parse) did not match the house's `屯門 Tuen Mun`: names with Han or kana characters were compared case-sensitively. They are now lowercased, and a name in two scripts matches by its Han part (`屯門` is `屯門 Tuen Mun`; `大阪` is still not `大坂`).
- **`Palestinian Territory`**, GeoNames' name, is written `Palestine` before parsing, which libpostal knows; and a lookup scoped to Palestine now searches the West Bank (`xw`), which the index files Palestine's places under. Gaza (`xg`) is not searched yet (a `ponytail:` in `Index::matching`).
- **A bare number read as a house name** (`13 Кӯчаи Каримов`, `87 Strada Principală`) is the house number when its part of the query is that number and the whole road; a road cut short (`strada ion` of `2 Strada Ion Druță`) keeps it a house name, which a Moldovan case needs.
- **A town and the country read as one house name** (`57 شارع دار المريسة, عين المريسة, Lebanon`) are the town and country, when the parse has a road and that one house name, and the last part names a country.
- **A spaced Eircode in its own part** (`…, Killarney, V93 RX97, Ireland`) is written without its space, which libpostal reads as a postcode instead of running the routing key into the road.
- **Bare calling codes** of countries whose postcodes have another number of digits or don't exist: Bhutan, Cambodia, Ecuador, Fiji, Haiti, Iraq, Myanmar, Nepal, Qatar, Syria, Turkmenistan, Yemen; a house's postcode `00977` is code 977.
- **A county named like the written town** confirms a house the completion lookup finds when the house has no town of its own (`Rue Joséphine Kengue 162, Montalier, Libreville`, which libpostal parses with `montalier` as the road).

SearchAddress 33,060 → 33,212 of 39,820 (+152: 28 Gabon, 28 Palestine, 27 Hong Kong, 14 Ireland, 12 Angola, 11 Tajikistan and smaller counts elsewhere), SuggestAddress +10 (Palestine), nothing newly failing; the 1,000 unscored address cases unchanged. Panel cases that fail search but pass suggest: 997 → 868. Two Hong Kong panel cases go from abstain to near miss: `8 逸東街 Yat Tung Street, 東涌 Tung Chung` answers house 8 of Yat Tung Street 0.28 km from the OSM point, which is the centroid of the Yat Tung Estate way that carries the address.

## What remains

Of the 1,065 training cases, 698 still fail: 387 at the parse, 209 in place corroboration, 52 at the street lookup and 50 at the house lookup. The groups with one cause are down to a few cases each (24 county cases whose road the parse loses, 19 postcode-before-country, 5 country-not-parsed, 4 calling-code, 3 Palestine). The rest are single cases or source quirks rather than resolver defects: houses numbered without a street (`2 1, Kampong Speu`), shop units (`Unit 207 Hermitage Mall`), U.S. Virgin Islands estates written as house names, P.O. boxes and placeholder postcodes (`00`, `0000`) next to the number, and Bahraini and Omani block and way numbers.
