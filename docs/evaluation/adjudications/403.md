## D: preserve the whole shop identifier

[OSM node 4675924775, version 5](https://www.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/node/4675924775/5) is at `(22.3740997, 113.9618378)` with:

```text
addr:housenumber=美樂花園商場58號鋪
addr:street=湖翠路 Wu Chui Road
addr:city=屯門 Tuen Mun
shop=bicycle
```

There is no `addr:unit`. The full compound number is present in the index at approximately `(22.374099120, 113.961837441)`; neither the bilingual Wu Chui Road document nor its uppercase English copy has civic number `58`. The Melody Garden building [w232197546](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/232197546) instead has civic number `2`; [mall relation 12990169](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/12990169) has no address tags.

The supported rule retains a Chinese mall/market name ending in `商場` or `街市`, its numeric/letter shop identifier and `號鋪`/`號舖` as one opaque lookup key, before or after the street. It never strips the mall or looks up civic `58` in its place. A synthetic street contains both a compound shop key and the corresponding civic number at different points, checking their separation and a missing shop. This is not a general mall hierarchy, indoor geocoder or arbitrary unit parser. An independently tagged shop/floor that is absent from the house-number index remains unavailable.

## Tung Sing Road: why 11A wins over 13

The two `global-osm-hk-6372574867` forms still fail ReverseGeocode on both sides. Coordinates below are `(latitude, longitude)`.

|---|---|---|---:|

| [OSM w680088383 v2](https://www.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/680088383/2), first vertex | 13 | `(22.2485378, 114.1552024)` | 3.42 m |
| Same building, computed polygon centroid | 13 | `(22.2485115023, 114.1551208737)` | 6.00 m |
| Current index, bilingual OSM street | 13 | `(22.2485127300, 114.1551482677)` | 3.18 m |
| Overture `8393023d-a670-4737-87a8-f3ee8a6950df` | 11A | `(22.2485300, 114.1551600)` | 2.50 m |
| Current index, separate source street document | 11a | `(22.2485294938, 114.1551589966)` | 2.55 m |

The Overture row attributes 11A to OpenAddresses `hk/countrywide-en`, record `26c9fb9cf0a86f53`, version `932101`; Overture has no number 13 on Tung Sing Road. These nearby sources disagree on the number, not merely the street spelling.

Before centroid handling, the node for 13 was west of the building's first vertex and won the same-rank duplicate ordering. The centroid is west of that node, changing the duplicate anchor; current duplicate handling averages nearby best-rank copies. The indexed 13 point is consistent with that averaging and quantization. ReverseGeocode compares distances across the separate street documents, so 11A wins by about 0.64 m, with no tie.

The historical transition is reconstructed from source geometry and duplicate ordering; incompatible historical indexes were not executed with the new consumer. Neither source proves surveyed entrance accuracy. Preferring OSM, pinning number 13 or redesigning duplicate import would be a different change, not a village parse repair.
